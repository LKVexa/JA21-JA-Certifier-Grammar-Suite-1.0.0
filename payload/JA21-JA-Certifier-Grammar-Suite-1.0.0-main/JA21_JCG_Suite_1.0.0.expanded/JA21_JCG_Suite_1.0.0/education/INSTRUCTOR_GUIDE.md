# Instructor Guide

## Teaching method

Use a three-record pattern: accepted, rejected, and certification/advanced-analysis. Require learners to trace source → AST → semantic interpretation → validator → R12 → MCRT rather than judging grammar text alone.

## Assessment rubric

- 30% grammar and semantic accuracy
- 20% diagnostic and failure-stage quality
- 20% policy, evidence, and certification reasoning
- 15% provenance and replay completeness
- 15% publication clarity

## Certification discipline

Keep the 60 certification records as a protected holdout set during instruction. Learners may study their metadata and profiles, but final assessment should require independent native reproduction rather than memorized expected outputs.
